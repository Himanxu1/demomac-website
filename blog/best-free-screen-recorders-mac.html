The 7 best free screen recorders for Mac, and the catch with each

You can record your Mac's screen for free, with no watermark, today — the tool is already installed. The question is what you need on top of that: system audio, a webcam, editing, polish. Every free screen recorder for Mac gives up something. Here's what each one gives up, so you can pick the catch you can live with.

Best ofBy Himanshu Singh · 20 October 2026 · 3 min read

1. macOS Screenshot toolbar — the one you already have

Catch: no system audio, no editing.

Press Shift-Command-5. Record the full screen or a selection, choose a microphone and a timer under Options, and optionally show mouse clicks. No watermark, no time limit.

2. QuickTime Player — the same, plus a trim

Catch: same as above.

File → New Screen Recording opens the same toolbar. You get QuickTime's trim and split afterwards, and a floating webcam window if you open a Movie Recording alongside.

3. OBS Studio — free with no limits

Catch: complex, and no editing at all.

Open source, no watermark, no time limit, records system audio on recent macOS with an audio capture source, and streams. Expect to spend half an hour on setup.

4. Cap — free with an editor

Catch: personal use only on the free plan.

Open source, one-click recording, and a Studio Mode editor with zooms and backgrounds. For commercial use you'll need the Desktop licence ($29/year or $58 lifetime).

5. Loom Starter — free with a share link

Catch: 5-minute recordings, 25 videos, everything uploaded.

Ideal for quick messages to a colleague. Not for anything you'll publish.

6. iMovie — free editing for any of the above

Catch: it's an editor, not a recorder.

Pair the built-in recorder with iMovie for trims, titles and music. Zooms are possible with the Ken Burns effect but fiddly.

7. ScreenToast's free tier — free polish, pay to export

Catch: exporting needs Pro ($59/year or $7/month).

Recording and editing are free with no time limit and no sign-in, including Auto Polish, which adds zooms, smooths the cursor and adds a background. It's the way to find out whether automatic polish is worth paying for before you spend a cent. We list it last because, honestly, it isn't free if you need the file.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free screen recorder for Mac?

For no limits at all, OBS Studio. For zero setup, the built-in recorder (Shift-Command-5). For a free recorder with an editor and zooms, Cap's free plan (personal use).

Is there a free screen recorder for Mac without a watermark?

Yes: the built-in recorder, QuickTime and OBS Studio never add a watermark. See our list of Mac screen recorders with no watermark.

Can I record internal audio for free on a Mac?

Yes, with OBS Studio, or with the built-in recorder plus a free virtual audio driver like BlackHole.
