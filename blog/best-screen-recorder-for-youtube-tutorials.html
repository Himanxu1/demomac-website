The best Mac screen recorders for YouTube tutorials

A tutorial channel lives or dies on watchability: text you can read on a phone, audio that doesn't sound like a laptop, a pace that doesn't drag. Picking the right screen recorder for YouTube takes care of most of that before you edit. Here are five Mac picks and what each is best for.

Best ofBy Himanshu Singh · 20 October 2026 · 2 min read

What a YouTube tutorial recorder needs

  • Zooms so code and menus are readable on a phone, where most YouTube is watched.
  • Separate audio tracks for your mic and the system, so you can fix one without the other.
  • Mic clean-up to remove hiss and room noise.
  • Webcam that you can move or remove after recording.
  • Cutting tools — trim, ripple delete, speed up the boring bits.
  • Shorts — a 9:16 version for YouTube Shorts.
  • Captions you can upload as an .srt file.

2. ScreenFlow — for long, heavily edited videos

If your channel's style is lots of cuts, b-roll and graphics, ScreenFlow's full timeline gives you control. $199 once.

3. Camtasia — for course-style channels

Huge asset library, animations and annotations; also runs on Windows. Yearly plans.

4. OBS Studio — for live and long recordings

If you stream your coding sessions or record hour-long sessions to edit later, OBS is free and reliable. Pair it with DaVinci Resolve. Free.

5. Screen Studio — for short, polished tutorials

Great automatic zooms and camera layouts. $108/year.

Tips for better tutorial recordings

  1. Increase your text size to 125% in your editor or browser before recording.
  2. Record in sections. Pause between steps; ScreenToast's pause never shows up in the final video.
  3. Use a 16:9 area rather than an ultrawide display, so YouTube doesn't letterbox you.
  4. Turn on Do Not Disturb and hide desktop icons.
  5. Upload captions as an .srt — it helps viewers and YouTube search.

Frequently asked questions

What resolution should I record YouTube tutorials in?

Record at your display's native resolution and export 1080p or 4K. More important than resolution is zooming in, so text is readable on a phone.

What do YouTubers use to screen record on Mac?

Common choices are ScreenFlow and Camtasia for heavy editing, OBS for streaming and raw capture, and Screen Studio or ScreenToast for polished tutorials with automatic zoom.

Can I make YouTube Shorts from a screen recording?

Yes. Record or export in 9:16. ScreenToast can lock the recording area to 9:16 and stack your camera above the screen for Shorts.
