Best for product demos

1. ScreenToast

A native Mac recorder (under 10 MB) built for demos. Stop recording and the editor opens polished: zooms on your clicks, bursts merged into one move, a smooth cursor, a background. It records in the shape you publish — lock an area to 9:16 for TikTok or 1:1 for LinkedIn — and drafts a narration script from what you did on screen. Captions are transcribed on your Mac, and it automatically blurs API keys and emails it finds in the recording. Nothing is ever uploaded. Free to record and edit; Pro is $59/year or $7/month to export.

2. Screen Studio

The app that popularised automatic zooms. Beautiful defaults, captions, transcript editing, camera layouts and hosted share links. $29/month or $108/year, subscription-only for new buyers, Mac-only.

Best for screenshots and short clips

3. CleanShot X

The best screenshot tool on the Mac, with screen recording (mic and system audio), GIF export, a video editor with smart zooms, and cloud links. $35 once with a year of updates.

Best for long tutorials and courses

4. ScreenFlow

A recorder bolted to a full multi-track editor. Every zoom and callout is manual, but nothing is out of reach. $199 once.

5. Camtasia

The long-standing course-builder's tool, with quizzes, SCORM export and a big asset library. Mac and Windows. Sold as yearly plans; the entry plan's editor watermarks exports.

Best for team communication

6. Loom

Record, get a link, done. Comments and view tracking for teams. Free with a 5-minute limit; Business $18/user/month. Every video is uploaded.

7. Tella

Browser-first recording and editing with nicer-looking results than a typical Loom. From $13/user/month billed yearly.

Best free screen recorders for Mac

8. Cap

Open source with an editor and zooms. Free for personal use; $58 lifetime for commercial use.

9. OBS Studio

Free, unlimited, and the standard for streaming. Steep learning curve, no editing.

10. macOS built-in recorder

Shift-Command-5. Free, instant, mic only — no system audio.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best screen recorder for Mac?

It depends on the job. For polished product demos: ScreenToast or Screen Studio. For screenshots plus short clips: CleanShot X. For full editing control: ScreenFlow. For free: the built-in recorder or OBS Studio.

Does Mac have a built-in screen recorder?

Yes. Press Shift-Command-5 to open the Screenshot toolbar, or use File > New Screen Recording in QuickTime Player. It records your mic but not system audio.

Which Mac screen recorder records internal audio?

ScreenToast, CleanShot X, Screen Studio, Cap and OBS Studio can all record system audio. The built-in macOS recorder can't.
