Better than QuickTime: screen recorders that fix what it can't do

QuickTime Player (and the Shift-Command-5 toolbar it shares a recorder with) is fine for a quick capture. But it has hard limits, and you've probably hit one. Here's each QuickTime limitation and the QuickTime screen recording alternatives that solve it.

Alternatives20 October 2026 · 3 min read

Limit 1: no system audio

QuickTime records your microphone only. If you're capturing a video, an app with sound, or a call, the result is silent. The free fix is a virtual audio driver like BlackHole plus a Multi-Output Device in Audio MIDI Setup — it works, but it's fiddly and easy to forget to switch back. Our guide walks through it.

Apps that record system audio directly: ScreenToast, CleanShot X, Screen Studio, OBS Studio (via an audio capture source), Cap.

Limit 2: no camera in the recording

QuickTime can't record your webcam and screen into one file. The workaround is a floating movie window, which you can't move or remove afterwards.

Better: ScreenToast records the camera on its own track, so after recording you can place it in any of eight spots, resize it, make it a circle, cut out the background behind you, or remove it entirely. It even slides out of the way when your cursor heads its way.

Limit 3: a wide video for a vertical world

QuickTime records a rectangle you drag freehand. Turning that into a 9:16 TikTok or Reel means cropping most of it away. ScreenToast locks the area to a publishing shape — 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 4:3 — while you drag. See vertical screen recording on Mac.

Limit 4: tiny text and a lost cursor

A full-screen recording watched on a laptop or phone makes text unreadable and the pointer hard to find. The fix is zooming in on the action. In QuickTime there's no zoom at all. Recorders like ScreenToast and Screen Studio zoom on your clicks automatically and smooth the cursor path. More on adding zoom.

Limit 5: editing is trim only

QuickTime can trim the ends, split clips and rotate. No cuts in the middle that close up neatly, no speed changes, no text, no blur, no captions. A recorder with a built-in editor saves you a trip to iMovie.

Limit 6: one export format

QuickTime saves a .mov. For a GIF, a small web MP4 or a platform preset, you need another tool. ScreenToast exports MP4, HEVC, GIF or ProRes with presets for YouTube, TikTok, Reels, LinkedIn, X and Product Hunt.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't QuickTime record sound from my Mac?

QuickTime and the Screenshot toolbar only record a microphone. They can't capture system audio — app sounds, browser video, call audio — without an extra virtual audio driver such as BlackHole.

Can QuickTime record my webcam and screen together?

Not in one recording. A common workaround is to open a New Movie Recording window, keep it floating in a corner, and record the screen with it visible.

What's the best free QuickTime alternative?

OBS Studio if you need system audio for free. Cap's free plan if you want light editing for personal videos.
