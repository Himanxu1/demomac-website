0.1.0 — 30 September 2026

First public release.

  • Recording — full screen, a single window, or an area locked to 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 4:3 or 21:9. Microphone, system audio and camera, each optional.
  • Auto Polish — runs on every recording: zooms driven by where the cursor actually went, a smoothed cursor path, click highlights, motion blur and a canvas.
  • Editor — canvas shape and resolution as separate choices, backgrounds, screen styling, zoom events, text overlays, privacy blur regions, camera bubble or split, split and trim, silence removal, undo and redo.
  • Voiceover — a draft script written from what you did on screen, spoken by ElevenLabs with your own API key.
  • Export — MP4, MOV and ProRes, ten presets, resolution capped at what was recorded so 4K never means an upscale.
  • Free until you export. Record and edit as much as you like; the licence is what lets you take the file out.
  • Universal binary — Apple silicon and Intel. macOS 14 or later.
Updates arrive in the app itself: ScreenToast → Check for Updates…, or automatically about once a day.
