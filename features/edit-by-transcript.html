Edit your screen recording by editing the text.

ScreenToast transcribes what you said while recording, on your Mac, and lets you edit the video through the words. Delete a sentence and the video cuts with it. Remove every "um" and long pause in one click.

Why edit by transcript

On a timeline, finding a fumbled sentence means scrubbing back and forth listening for it. In a transcript, you just read. For talking-heavy demos and tutorials, it turns an hour of cutting into a few minutes of deleting text.

What you can do

  • Delete a word or sentence and that part of the video is cut. Everything after it closes up, with zooms, captions and audio staying in step.
  • Remove filler words, every "um" and "uh", in one click.
  • Tighten long pauses so the demo keeps moving.
  • Fix the transcript so the captions spell your product's name right.
  • Undo anything. Your original recording is never changed. Edits sit on top, so a cut you regret can be put back.

Pairs well with

  • Speed-ups: play every stretch of typing at 3× with one command, and drop silent stretches entirely.
  • Captions: the same transcript becomes captions in four styles, and an .srt file.

Private by design

Transcription runs on your Mac. Your recording isn't uploaded to make the transcript.
