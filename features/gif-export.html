Presets for every platform

Ready-made settings for YouTube (1080p and 4K), TikTok, Reels, LinkedIn, X, Product Hunt and the web, or set your own size and frame rate. Exports are capped at what you recorded, so 4K never means an upscale.

Re-framed, not just letterboxed

Switch a wide recording to vertical or square and ScreenToast re-frames the picture to follow the action, instead of chopping the sides off or adding black bars.

Fast, and stays on your Mac

Exports use your Mac's own video hardware, so they finish quickly, and they're saved straight to your Mac. No upload, no queue, no link that quietly expires.
