ScreenToast / Features / Screen and webcam

Record your screen and webcam, and decide the layout later.

ScreenToast records your camera on its own track, next to the screen. After you stop, put yourself in a bubble, side by side with the screen, or full frame. Cut out the room behind you, or take the camera out entirely.

Layouts you can switch between

  • Bubble: in any of eight spots, in your choice of shape and size. When your cursor heads towards it, it slides to another corner so it never covers what you're showing.
  • Side by side: you and the screen together.
  • Camera only: for an intro or outro where you talk to the viewer.
  • Screen only: hide the camera for a section.

Switch layouts part-way through: camera only for the opening, a bubble during the demo, side by side for the wrap-up.

Look your best

  • Background removal: cut yourself out from the room behind you, on your Mac, no green screen needed.
  • Face tracking keeps you framed if you move.
  • Twelve colour looks, or load your own LUT.
  • In sync: camera and microphone line up with the picture.

Built for vertical video

Squeeze a desktop recording with a webcam corner into 9:16 and both become unreadable. ScreenToast can stack your face above the screen for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, so each gets a proper share of the frame. More on vertical screen recording.
