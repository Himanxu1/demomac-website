Screen Studio too expensive? 6 cheaper alternatives

Screen Studio is a lovely app with a price that adds up: $108 a year, or $348 a year if you pay month to month. If you record a few demos a month, that's a lot for zooms and a smooth cursor. Here are six cheaper Screen Studio alternatives for Mac, with the real two-year cost of each so you can compare like for like.

What Screen Studio costs in 2026

On its website (checked 20 October 2026), Screen Studio lists two plans with the same features:

  • Monthly: $29 a month — $696 over two years.
  • Yearly: $9 a month billed yearly — $216 over two years.

The old one-time licence isn't sold to new buyers any more, and there's no free plan. Students and educators get 40% off.

Cheapest isn't automatically best — what matters is how much editing each tool saves you. Here's what you get for the money.

1. ScreenToast — about half the price, more automation

$59 a year ($4.92/month) or $7 month to month. Recording and editing are free forever; you pay when you export.

That last point is the money-saver: you can record your demo, let Auto Polish place the zooms and smooth the cursor, edit it to perfection, and only pay if you're happy with the result. If you only publish in bursts — say, around launches — Pro Monthly at $7 for the month you're exporting is cheaper still.

On top of the zooms and cursor, the price includes a narration script drafted from your recording, on-device captions, automatic hiding of API keys and emails, and area recording locked to 9:16 for vertical video.

2. Cap — cheapest paid licence

$29 a year or $58 lifetime for the Desktop licence, which is what you need for commercial use. The free plan is personal-use only.

Cap is open source and its Studio Mode has zooms, backgrounds and an editor. It takes more hands-on editing than Screen Studio, but at $58 for life it's hard to argue with.

3. CleanShot X — cheapest if you also want screenshots

$35 once, including a year of updates; $19 a year after that if you want new versions.

CleanShot X replaces your screenshot tool too, and its video editor has smart zooms. It's lighter than Screen Studio for long demos, but for short clips it's excellent value.

4. FocuSee — cheaper yearly, works on Windows

Listed at $49.99 a year (Standard) or $199.99 lifetime (Advanced).

Auto zoom, cursor effects and a webcam overlay, on both Mac and Windows. Good value if you need both platforms.

5. Screen Charm — pay once

$99 once at its current launch price (listed down from $149), for up to 3 Macs.

It costs more than a year of the others, but if you'll use it for three years or longer, a one-time licence wins. Feature-wise it's close to Screen Studio: auto zoom, smooth cursor, captions, vertical export.

6. OBS Studio — free, if you'll do the editing

OBS costs nothing and records beautifully, but it polishes nothing. Pair it with a free editor and you can get there — it just costs you time instead of money.

How to cut the cost of any of these

  • Pay monthly only when you're publishing. If you make demos around launches, a month here and there beats a yearly plan.
  • Check student and education discounts. Screen Studio offers 40% off for students and educators.
  • Try before you pay. Record the same demo in two apps. The one that needs less fixing is the cheaper one, whatever the price tag.

Our pick: if you want Screen Studio's result with less effort and less money, ScreenToast at $59 a year is the closest match. If you only need the lowest number, Cap's $58 lifetime licence wins.
