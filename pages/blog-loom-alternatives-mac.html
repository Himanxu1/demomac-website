7 Loom alternatives for Mac, including ones that never upload

Loom is the default for quick async video, but three things send people looking for a Loom alternative on Mac: the 5-minute limit on the free plan, the per-user price once a team adopts it, and the fact that every recording goes to the cloud. Here are seven alternatives, grouped by which of those problems they solve.

First: what kind of video are you making?

Loom is built for messages — a quick walkthrough you send and forget. If that's you, you want another link-based tool. If you're actually making content — a demo for your website, a tutorial, a launch video — you've outgrown Loom, and a polishing recorder will serve you better.

If you want a cheaper link-based tool

1. Tella

Pro is $13 per user per month billed yearly. Tella records on Mac, Windows or in the browser, and its editor makes videos look more produced than a typical Loom: layouts, backgrounds, clips you can trim. Best when a polished link is the deliverable.

2. Cap

Open source, Mac and Windows, with "instant mode" share links like Loom's. The free plan is personal use only with 5-minute links; Cap Pro is $12 per user per month ($8.16 billed yearly) with unlimited cloud.

3. CleanShot X

$35 once, including 1 GB of cloud. Record a clip, get a link. A great fit if you also want a top-tier screenshot tool and your videos are short.

If you want your videos to stay on your Mac

4. ScreenToast

ScreenToast never uploads anything — there's no account and no cloud. It's built for videos you publish rather than send: when you stop recording, it places zooms on your clicks, smooths the cursor, and gives you an editor with captions transcribed on your Mac. Your camera sits in a bubble that slides away from your cursor, or stacks above the screen for vertical video. Free to record and edit; $59 a year to export.

5. ScreenFlow

$199 once. A full editor and recorder for long-form tutorials, all local.

6. OBS Studio

Free and open source, records locally with no limits. No editing or sharing — just capture.

7. The macOS built-in recorder

Press Shift-Command-5. Free, local, records your mic — but not system audio. For a camera bubble, open QuickTime's movie recording window and keep it in the corner of your screen.

When Loom is still the right answer

If your team already lives in Loom, comments and view notifications matter to you, and your videos are internal, the switching cost may not be worth it. Loom is excellent at what it's for.

Cheaper links: Tella or Cap. Privacy and polish: ScreenToast. Free and local: OBS or the built-in recorder.
