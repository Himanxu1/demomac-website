OBS alternatives for Mac, for when you just want to record your screen

OBS Studio is one of the best free programs ever made — for streaming. For a screen recording, it means scenes, sources, encoders, output settings and an audio mixer before you've recorded a second. If you've searched for an OBS alternative for Mac, you probably want the opposite: click record, get a good-looking video. Here are the best options.

When OBS is still the right tool

Stay with OBS if you're live streaming, compositing several sources into one scene, recording for hours, or need a free recorder with no restrictions at all. Nothing on this list replaces those.

Simpler free alternatives

1. macOS Screenshot toolbar

Press Shift-Command-5, choose full screen or a selection, pick your mic under Options, and click Record. Zero setup. The limitation: it doesn't record system audio. See how to screen record on a Mac.

2. QuickTime Player

The same recorder, plus a simple trim and the ability to show your webcam in a floating window. Free and built in.

3. Cap (free plan)

Open source, one-click recording, and an editor with backgrounds and zooms. Personal use only on the free plan.

Simpler paid alternatives that also polish

4. ScreenToast

One recorder bar: pick Display, Window or Area, toggle camera, mic and system audio, hit record. Stop with ⌘⇧2 from anywhere, and the editor opens with zooms already placed and the cursor smoothed. Mic, system audio and camera stay on separate tracks. Free to record and edit, $59 a year to export.

5. Screen Studio

The same simple flow with polished automatic results. $108 a year.

6. CleanShot X

Records screen with mic and system audio from the menu bar, plus the best screenshot tool on the Mac. $35 once.
