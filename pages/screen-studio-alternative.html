Where ScreenToast is the better fit

You don't want another subscription

One year of Screen Studio ($108, billed up front) costs nearly twice ScreenToast's one-time price, and the year after costs the same again. ScreenToast is $59 once for up to three Macs, every future update included, with a 14-day refund if it isn't for you.

You post vertical video

Cropping a wide recording into 9:16 throws most of the picture away. ScreenToast lets you record an area locked to 9:16 (or 1:1, 4:5, 4:3, 16:9) so what you film is exactly what you post, and the editor opens already set up for TikTok and Reels.

You hate writing the script

ScreenToast reads what happened in your recording — the clicks, the app switches, the shortcuts — and drafts narration that follows along, in the style you pick: product demo, launch clip, tutorial, pitch or social. You edit the words; you don't start from a blank box.

Your recordings shouldn't leave your Mac

There's no account and no upload. Recording, editing and exporting all happen on your Mac. The only exception is spoken narration, where the approved text — never the video — goes to ElevenLabs.

Where Screen Studio is the better fit

Being straight about this saves you a refund:

  • You share by link. Screen Studio gives you a hosted link; ScreenToast saves a file.
  • You want built-in AI voices without another account. ScreenToast's spoken narration needs your own ElevenLabs API key.
  • You rely on camera background removal or face tracking. ScreenToast's camera is a simpler bubble or split layout.

What switching looks like

  1. Download ScreenToast — 7.7 MB, no account. Grant Screen Recording permission when macOS asks.
  2. Record the same demo you'd record in Screen Studio. Whole display, one window, or an area in the shape you publish.
  3. Let Auto Polish do its pass, then drag, retime or delete any zoom it placed.
  4. Export for the platform you post to. That's the only point you're asked to pay.
