Privacy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Your recordings never leave your Mac. ScreenToast records, edits and exports entirely on your computer. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud, and no upload. We cannot see your recordings because they are never sent anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. ScreenToast contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no usage tracking. We do not know how many times you open it or what you record.

The three times the app talks to the internet

All three are listed here because "we don't collect anything" is only meaningful if we also say what does leave the machine.

1. Buying a licence

Payment is handled by Dodo Payments, who act as Merchant of Record. Checkout happens on their site, in your browser. They collect the billing details a payment requires — name, email, address, tax region — and process the card. ScreenToast never sees or stores card details. Dodo's privacy policy governs that data: dodopayments.com/privacy.

2. Activating a licence

When you paste a licence key, the app sends that key and a machine name to Dodo so the three-Mac limit can be counted. That is the whole request. The key is then stored in your Mac's Keychain. Deactivating a Mac removes the seat.

3. Generating a voiceover — optional

Only if you use the voiceover feature, and only with an ElevenLabs API key you supply yourself. The text of the script you wrote is sent to ElevenLabs, which returns audio. Your video and your screen contents are never sent — only the words. Your API key is stored in your Keychain and used to talk to ElevenLabs directly; it never reaches us. ElevenLabs' privacy policy governs that text: elevenlabs.io/privacy.

Checking for updates

About once a day the app fetches appcast.xml from screentoast.com to see whether a newer version exists. This is an ordinary web request: our host records the IP address and app version, as any web server does. No identifier, licence key or usage data is attached, and you can turn the check off in the app.

Screen recording permission

macOS asks for Screen Recording, Microphone and Camera permission because a screen recorder cannot work without them. They are used only to capture what you asked to capture, while you are recording. You can revoke them at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For the billing data Dodo holds, contact them, or write to us and we will pass it on.

Questions about any of this: hello@screentoast.com.
